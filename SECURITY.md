# Security policy

Yearprint's fairness depends on players not being able to see an answer early. Please treat any of these as a security issue:

- Reading an edition's answer year, its reveal notes or an unpaid inside section before your game ends.
- Reading an edition that isn't live yet (a draft, or one dated in the future).
- Changing your score, your guesses or another player's data.
- Any way into other users' accounts or personal data.

## Reporting

Please **don't open a public issue.** Report it privately through GitHub instead: go to the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**.

Include what you did, what you could see or change, and steps to reproduce it. You'll get a reply as soon as possible, and a fix goes out before any details are made public.
